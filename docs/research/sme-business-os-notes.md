# SME Business OS Research Notes

## Research Purpose

Collect domain notes for building WatchDog-BMS as an SME Business OS.

## Key Domain Areas

- SME operations
- Inventory management
- Sales and CRM
- Accounting support
- Owner dashboard and KPI
- Notifications and workflow automation
- AI assistant for business operations

## Initial Hypotheses

1. SME owners need visibility more than complex ERP features at the beginning.
2. Inventory accuracy and sales follow-up are high-value early workflows.
3. Accounting should start as structured records and export support before full compliance automation.
4. Web-first workflows plus LINE AI summaries are essential for daily users.
5. Web admin is still needed for setup, reports, permissions, and bulk operations.
6. AI is most useful first as a read-only summarizer and business analyst.

## Inventory Questions

- Should negative stock be blocked or allowed with warning?
- Is multi-location stock required in MVP?
- Do target users need barcode support early?
- How should stock adjustment approvals work?
- What inventory valuation method is required later?

## CRM Questions

- Is the main workflow lead pipeline, customer follow-up, or repeat purchase tracking?
- What fields are essential for customer profile?
- Should tasks be assigned to sales users?
- How should duplicate customer records be handled?

## Accounting Questions

- Should MVP support invoice drafts only or actual invoice records?
- Which tax rules matter for the first target market?
- Should accounting periods be lockable?
- What export format do accountants need?
- What must be auditable for finance users?

## AI Assistant Questions

- Which data can AI access by default?
- Should AI be read-only in MVP?
- How should AI cite sources in answers?
- What should AI do when data is incomplete?
- Which actions require explicit approval?
