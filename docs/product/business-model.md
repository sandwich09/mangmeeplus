# Business Model Draft

## Product Category

SME Business OS combining ERP-lite, CRM, inventory, Thai tax, FlowAccount-style accounting, reimbursement, notifications, KPI dashboards, workflow automation, and AI Business Assistant.

## Target Customers

### Primary

- Small retail businesses
- Trading companies
- Distribution businesses
- Service businesses with stock or customer pipeline
- E-commerce sellers using Shopee, TikTok Shop, Facebook, or similar channels
- Owner-led SMEs that currently use spreadsheets and chat apps

### Secondary

- Accountants managing SME clients
- Consultants implementing internal business systems
- Small teams that outgrow basic POS or spreadsheet workflows

## Value Proposition

WatchDog-BMS helps SMEs centralize daily operations, reduce blind spots, and make faster decisions through a full web workspace, unified business data, proactive LINE alerts, Thai tax readiness, and AI summaries.

## Pain Points Solved

- Stock mismatch and unclear inventory movement
- Manual sales and customer follow-up tracking
- Delayed KPI visibility
- Missed tax deadlines or unclear tax documents
- Manual expense/reimbursement document collection, category sorting, and approval tracking
- Weak owner oversight when team grows
- Sharing owner/admin credentials when employees need different access
- Repeated manual reporting
- Unclear marketplace/channel performance
- Business data scattered across tools

## Possible Pricing Models

### SaaS Subscription

- Starter: owner plus small team, web dashboard, LINE AI summaries, inventory, CRM, and basic documents
- Growth: accounting documents, tax reports, reimbursement, approvals, dashboards, automation
- Pro: advanced AI agents, multi-branch, marketplace integrations, advanced reports

### Add-ons

- AI assistant quota
- Accounting export/integration
- Thai tax report pack
- Reimbursement approval workflow
- Marketplace integration pack
- Advanced inventory forecasting
- AI marketing content pack
- Multi-branch operations
- Employee/role access pack
- Custom onboarding or migration

## Go-to-market Ideas

- Start with one industry niche to reduce complexity
- Offer spreadsheet migration as onboarding hook
- Build templates for common SME workflows
- Use owner dashboard, LINE expense capture, tax-evidence review, and accountant-ready web evidence/export views as the main demo
- Partner with accountants or operations consultants
- Demonstrate LINE AI assistant with real examples: sales today, low stock, overdue invoice, tax reminder, and campaign draft, all linking back to web details

## Success Metrics

- Daily active business users
- Number of transactions recorded per business
- Inventory accuracy improvement
- Follow-up completion rate
- Report generation frequency
- LINE alert-to-web action completion time
- Tax reminder accuracy and on-time preparation rate
- AI chat usage by owner/team role
- Percentage of shipped read features and golden business questions answered correctly in LINE without web handoff
- Wrong-subject tool rate and repeated-clarification rate in LINE AI
- Active named employee users by role
- Time from employee creation to first web login or LINE link
- Marketplace campaign draft usage
- Time saved from manual reporting
- Churn by business segment

## Open Business Questions

- Which SME niche should be first target?
- Which Thai SME segment should be first: retail, trading, service, or e-commerce?
- Which FlowAccount-style accounting features must be first: sales documents, expenses, tax, payroll, or bank reconciliation?
- Which integrations are required earliest?
- What pricing is acceptable for Thai SMEs or target market?
- Should AI be bundled or metered separately?
- Which employee/user count belongs in each pricing tier?
