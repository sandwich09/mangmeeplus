## Objections (Codex dispute log)

### 2026-07-10 — T1: the prescribed gate is too early to prevent a stale/in-flight autonomous mutation

- **Claim:** T1's planned `evaluate()` gate plus a fresh read at retry load time does not fully achieve the handoff's business promise that a late per-conversation toggle prevents any stale queued autonomous reply. The common action path needs a fresh deterministic guard at the final mutation boundary after awaited AI work; the immediate in-process job also needs to stop using its captured conversation snapshot as gate authority.
- **Evidence:** The immediate durable job closes over the conversation loaded before enqueue and passes that same object into the background attempt (`apps/api/src/modules/crm-inbox/application/use-cases/process-inbound-line-message.use-case.ts:209-218`). The retry path does read once at context load (`process-inbound-line-message.use-case.ts:223-251`), but `evaluate()` can then await context, intake, reply-draft, staff/branch, availability, and booking-extract work (`apps/api/src/modules/crm-inbox/application/use-cases/crm-ai-auto-reply.use-case.ts:52-114`, `:127-155`). After evaluation, the common path creates a booking or sends an AI/holding message without re-reading the conversation flag (`process-inbound-line-message.use-case.ts:304-350`); the outbound row and LINE call happen directly at `:401-434`. The autonomous booking write is likewise immediate at `apps/api/src/modules/crm/application/use-cases/crm-booking.use-case.ts:142-153`.
- **Business impact:** If staff turns AI off after the webhook enqueues/starts the job, or while intake/draft/booking extraction is in flight, an enabled snapshot can still create a booking and/or send an AI or holding message. That is exactly the stale autonomous traffic the one-sentence business goal and T1 intent say must not slip through.
- **Smallest correct alternative:** Keep the planned early `evaluate()` gate for cheap denial and `conversation_disabled` audit, but refresh the tenant-scoped conversation in the common job path before evaluation and re-read it once more after `evaluate()` immediately before the send/book/holding branches. If the final row is missing, fail the job consistently; if disabled, audit `conversation_disabled`, finish the job without any outbound row, LINE call, or booking mutation, and use the fresh row for permitted actions. Extend the real use-case spec with a deferred evaluation that flips the in-memory row before resolution and proves zero reply, push, holding message, and booking; retain the planned retry-flip and enabled consent/cap regression cases. This is two shared-path reads, no new abstraction or dependency, and closes both the captured-snapshot and long-running-evaluation windows.
