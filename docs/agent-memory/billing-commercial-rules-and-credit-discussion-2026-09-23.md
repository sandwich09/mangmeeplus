# Agent Memory: commercial package rules and upcoming credit discussion

Status: 80-credit CHAT default implemented in workspace on 2026-09-23; focused tests passed. Await API typecheck/UAT and deployment.

Scope: package upgrade, repeat/full package stacking, advance auto-renewing deals, special sales prices, and the pending credit discussion.

## Confirmed product decisions

- An active one-time STARTER package with 30 receipts, one used, upgraded to BASIC on the same Bangkok purchase day for the full 250-baht tier difference gets 20 additional receipts, leaving 49; its paid-through date stays the same. Later upgrades prorate by paid service time with half-up integer receipt rounding.
- To have 79 remaining in that example, the customer purchases a separate full-price BASIC package for 499 baht: 29 unused STARTER receipts carry, 50 BASIC receipts become available after verified payment, and expiry extends one month from the old paid-through. Total customer payments in the example are 748 baht. No implicit full-pack grant or old-price credit is attached to the 250-baht upgrade.
- Repeating the same full package charges its full catalog price, gives its full receipt allowance after verified payment, and extends from the old paid-through. A collectible automatic renewal must move to the new expiry so the customer is not charged twice for overlapping time.
- For an approved auto-renewing sales deal bought while a one-time package is active, charge the first deal installment at acceptance; keep old rights until their paid-through; activate deal rights then; collect the next installment only when the first deal cycle ends. Do not remove `BILLING_MODE_CHANGE_PENDING` alone. Spec §19.10 records this decision, but the safe end-to-end transition is still pending implementation.

## Commercial policy proposed, not yet approved

- “Operator wins first” should mean positive expected contribution after payment fees, OCR/AI and other variable costs, support, sales incentives, and refund/dispute reserve; for an existing paying customer, also compare with expected catalog renewal or lapse. Early cash for a future deal cycle is not profit earned for that cycle.
- A sales deal at about 613 baht against a 999-baht catalog cycle is a 38.6% reduction. This is an example under review, not an approved default or proof of profit. Actual cost and retention data are needed to decide whether it wins.
- Consider an enforceable deal price/contribution floor calculated from the deal's custom limits and features, plus explicit approval/audit rules for exceptions and a finite discount cycle count. Current approved sales-deal rule allows prices down to 1 satang with a warning, so imposing a floor requires a deliberate Owner spec amendment; do not silently change it.
- Existing one-time sales deals and CARD_AUTO deal conversion may start deal rights immediately under their current rules; their treatment of remaining paid time differs from the deferred auto-renewing deal. Resolve this deliberately before unifying their economic treatment.

## Credit: questions reserved for the next discussion

- Define which “credit” is meant: unused paid package value, unused receipt allowance, AI spending credit, wallet/store credit, sales commission credit, or another balance. These have different ledgers and expiry rules; do not combine them by name alone.
- If credit means monetary value from unused paid time, decide eligibility by purchase/deal mode, valuation basis (actual paid amount after discounts and tax treatment versus catalog price), start/end timestamps, rounding, expiry, refund/dispute treatment, and whether it may reduce a later upgrade, full pack, or renewal invoice.
- Decide whether a credit is a customer benefit, a liability carried to a later cycle, or a sales incentive; define the operator contribution floor after the credit and prevent the same paid period from being credited and retained as usable rights.
- Decide what the quote and receipt show, how double redemption is prevented, and how account, provider, and audit ledgers reconcile. Preserve §19.10's no-overlap billing and delayed rights until the credit rule is explicit.

## AI credit economics discussion (2026-09-23)

- Owner clarified that the 80-baht amount under discussion is intended for **both trial and paid packages**. This clarifies the package contexts, but does not yet approve a new live configuration, paid-tier credit schedule, or contribution floor.
- The approved trial wording spec (`docs/specs/line-ai-credit-wording-and-deterministic-reads-spec-th.md` §7) describes **80 baht/month for CHAT plus a separate 15-receipt OCR entitlement**, with a planning OCR money guard of about 60 baht; its planned total is 140 baht/month. It explicitly says this allocation is not an automatic default for trial or paid companies and needs an audited admin setting. The older LINE signup spec's 80-baht trial budget is a shared total/stop cap; do not treat those two designs as already equivalent in production.
- The paid catalog spec (`docs/specs/mangmee-sales-deals-and-locked-signup-spec-th.md` §20.1) sets **no per-tier AI credit**: paid packages inherit the platform default, and a sales deal may override it. Code/config currently defaults `AI_MONTHLY_BUDGET_SATANG` to 200,000 satang (2,000 baht) absent DB/env overrides. Live effective company/platform values were not read. Confirm them before claiming all paying customers currently receive an 80-baht cap.
- Historical production meter in the LINE signup spec: receipt through LINE about 2.13 baht average / 3.05 baht p90 before the intake optimization; chat about 0.78 baht p50. The newer wording spec estimates about 2.8 baht p90 per receipt after optimization, and applies a 1.3 reread/multipage factor for an OCR planning guard. These are planning inputs, not a guarantee or a current AWS invoice reconciliation.
- Stress test if **CHAT 80 baht/month plus OCR guard `receiptAllowance × 2.8 × 1.3`** is promised for every paid tier: for monthly STARTER/BASIC/PRO/ENTERPRISE, OCR guards are 109.20/182/364/1,820 baht. At catalog prices 249/499/999/1,999 and a domestic-card Stripe fee of 3.65% + 10 baht, the remaining contribution before infrastructure, support, sales compensation and reserves is about 40.71/208.79/508.54/16.04 baht per month. This assumes full use of both caps, so it is a stress case rather than expected spend. [Stripe Thailand pricing](https://stripe.com/th/pricing).
- Annual pricing is ten monthly prices while annual receipt allowance is twelve monthly allowances granted immediately. Under the same full-utilization stress case and one domestic-card charge, annual STARTER leaves about 118.71 baht for the **whole year** and annual ENTERPRISE loses about 3,549.63 baht before other costs. A monthly OCR guard based on the nominal monthly count can also block early use of the annual receipt allowance, contradicting its upfront grant; annual money/quantity policy needs an explicit design.
- A PRO sales price of about 613 baht per month plus the above 80-baht CHAT and 100-receipt OCR allowance leaves about 136.63 baht before other costs after the domestic-card fee. Price floors must use deal-specific AI caps and receipt allowance, not just the catalog tier name. Never deduct OCR twice from the same AI cost meter.
- Operator-first proposed test: `net collected revenue after tax/payment fee − actual or protected AI/OCR spend − other variable/support/sales/refund reserve ≥ required contribution`. For a quote, use protected spend/approved utilization scenario; for actual profitability, use settled meter and realized conversion/retention. This is an analysis rule, not an approved margin percentage.
- Open for owner decision: whether 80 baht is the CHAT cap or the TOTAL cap on paid packages; how much OCR money to reserve per paid tier and annual period; whether the customer-facing receipt allowance promises automatic AI reading of every receipt; and the minimum contribution or margin that sales deals must preserve.
- Proposed lifecycle rule to reconcile with the earlier package decisions: an upgrade paid by difference must not reissue a whole new month's AI credit when the old paid period continues; a separate full-price purchase that extends paidThrough can attach the next credit entitlement to its new service period instead of immediately refilling the current one; an advance auto-renewing deal's credit starts when deal rights start, not when the first installment is collected. Treat trial AI spend as acquisition cost when a shop converts, and do not transform unused trial AI credit into a cash discount. These are proposals requiring a period ledger and explicit owner approval in the future spec.

### Chat-only economics, before setting receipt policy

- Owner directed analysis of **CHAT credit alone** first; receipt reading is accounted for by successfully read receipt units separately. Do not charge estimated OCR cost against the chat-credit budget or use an OCR guard to answer whether the 80-baht CHAT cap itself is viable.
- Cost per chat must include the entire text path, including `line-intake-route` plus any `line-turn` plan/loop/compose calls. The 2026-09-17 benchmark (`docs/research/line-ai-intake-cost-benchmark-2026-09-17.md` §10.1) measured 0.62 baht per successful text task over a synthetic mixed text corpus after the intake change. Its 2026-09-18 R5 business-read subset (§14) measured 0.75 baht per successful read after deterministic responses. These are different synthetic workloads, not current production percentiles; cold caches, long chains and timeouts can cost more. The historical production `line-turn` average of 0.74 baht per call is **not** a complete turn price.
- Illustrations using the two benchmark rates: 20 text turns cost about 12.40–15 baht, 50 about 31–37.50, 100 about 62–75. A historical shop sample reported 17 chat turns in August; at these benchmark rates that would cost about 10.54–12.75 baht, but this is a cross-source illustration rather than a measured present-day per-shop bill. An 80-baht monthly cap is maximum exposure, not an automatic 80-baht charge.
- Monthly card sale stress test at full CHAT cap and Stripe domestic-card standard 3.65% + 10 baht: STARTER 249 leaves 149.91 baht, BASIC 499 leaves 390.79, PRO 999 leaves 872.54, ENTERPRISE 1,999 leaves 1,836.04, and illustrative special PRO price 613 leaves 500.63, **before receipt economics and every other cost**. Annual STARTER 2,490 with one card charge leaves about 119.09 baht per month after allocating 80 baht CHAT across 12 months, again before other costs. [Stripe pricing](https://stripe.com/th/pricing).
- Provisional business conclusion: 80 baht as a hard CHAT cap appears affordable within every listed paid sticker price and is likely rarely exhausted at the historical 17-turn usage level. This does not establish package profitability or free-trial acquisition ROI. To make an operator-first rule, measure production per-company monthly CHAT actual/p50/p90/p99 (including every tag and settled hold), paid retention/conversion, and non-chat unit economics; sales-deal approval should use the actual deal price and CHAT cap. No live meter pull or policy change in this analysis.
